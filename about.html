06 — ABOUT THE STUDIO

We don't just build gardens.
We engineer living places.

Premium Fencing & Landscaping is a small Melbourne studio with a simple obsession: outdoor spaces that are as considered as the homes they surround. Two founders, one crew, and nineteen seasons of turning bare blocks into places people actually live in.

OUR STORY

Built on craft,
grown on trust.

We started Premium because too many fences and gardens were being thrown together — fast, flat, and forgotten within a season. We wanted to work the other way: measure twice, plant once, and treat a backyard with the same rigour as a build.

Every project begins on the land itself — reading the light, the slope, the soil and the way you actually want to live outside. Only then do we draw. The result is fencing that frames, planting that matures into something richer each year, and lawns engineered to last.

We're not the biggest studio in Melbourne, and we don't want to be. Staying small is how we keep the founders on every site and the standard exactly where it should be.
